Skip to workspace

Search → Review → Revise

Keep this workspace and your research database open in separate tabs. Record your work as you go. This tool creates your research-process report; it does not search for articles.

Choose any tab to open its section. You can move between tabs at any time; completing a section is not required to navigate. Use your Canvas assignment for source requirements and submission instructions. Keep responses concise and specific.

How to use and save this workspace

Follow your course assignment for source requirements and submission instructions. This tool organizes your work; it does not perform searches or verify sources.

Work is saved locally in this browser when available. It is not sent to your instructor or a server. Browser clearing, private browsing, or changing devices can remove access. Save a project backup regularly; it includes your text and uploaded images. Loading a backup replaces the current workspace.

The PDF button opens a report preview. Choose Print / Save PDF, select Save as PDF in your browser, and turn off browser headers and footers for a clean report. Open the tool in a new tab if printing from Canvas is unavailable.

Work saves in this browser as you type. Save a backup before leaving.

Tabs change the section shown below. Your entries stay in place when you switch sections. Feedback prompts still require a documented first search.

Write a starting question related to your professional interests. It can change as you learn; do not assume the intervention will help.

Label the population (who), intervention or exposure (what), comparison (compared with what), and outcome (what is measured). Write “Not applicable” for a component that does not fit.

List one main idea followed by alternative terms. Example format: Resistance training: strength training, weight training.

List a different main idea and alternative terms. Example format: Sleep: sleep quality.

Use this only if a third idea is useful. Leave blank otherwise.

3. Consider feedback

Complete your first-search record, evidence, and observations before requesting prompts. The tool checks a few search features and offers general troubleshooting questions. It does not interpret your observations or screenshots, verify articles, or grade your work.

Prompts appear here after your first attempt is saved. Choose one relevant prompt for your response below; leave this record intact. You can add instructor or peer feedback.
